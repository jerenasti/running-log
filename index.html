No activities loaded

Start the app with start.bat to load your Garmin data automatically, or upload a CSV export:

  1. Open Garmin Connect in your browser and sign in.
  2. Choose Activities, then All Activities.
  3. Scroll down until all the activities you want are visible. The export only includes rows loaded in the list.
  4. Choose Export CSV at the top of the page.
  5. Drop the file here, or choose it with the Upload CSV file button below.

The file is processed only in your own browser. It is not sent anywhere.
